          How it works

          Ratings follow the Cook Political Report unless a shift names another forecaster. Polls are public surveys listed by RealClearPolling and news outlets. Poll closing times are for the last polls in each state and can vary by county. Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, gathers and summarizes the news each morning. Check the linked sources before you rely on any figure.
